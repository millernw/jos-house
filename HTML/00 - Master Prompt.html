Project Identity

  • Project name: Jo's House
  • Tagline: Find Rest. Feel Like Yourself Again.
  • Logo subtitle: A Place of Peace & Healing
  • Organization type: 501(c)(3) nonprofit
  • Address: 10469 S. SR5, Whitley County, Indiana 46787
  • Website: joshouse.com
  • One-liner: Jo's House is the only bee bed therapy center in northeast Indiana — a place where worn-down, stressed, and grieving people lie above active beehives for 45 minutes and walk out feeling genuinely different.
  • Primary goal: Book sessions (primary CTA) and capture email leads via a free course download (transitional CTA for visitors not ready to book)
  • Success metric: Sessions booked and free course downloads

Audience

  • Primary visitor: A worn-down woman, typically 30s–50s, who has tried the medications, the apps, the therapy, and the massages — and still can't find genuine rest. She is not in crisis. She is quietly exhausted and quietly afraid that real rest just isn't available to her.
  • Her exact words: "I've tried everything." "My mind won't shut off." "Maybe I'm just not someone who gets to feel calm."
  • Secondary visitor: People walking through grief — especially those who have lost someone to suicide, or who are carrying PTSD, high anxiety, or loss that doesn't show up on a doctor's chart. Bryan has a specific heart for this group. They should see themselves on this site.
  • Device context: Primarily mobile — locals discovering the site via social or word of mouth
  • Tech literacy: General consumer — keep everything simple and clear

Ground Rules for This Project

  • Bryan is the guide. The visitor is the hero. Never position Jo's House as the hero of its own story.
  • Always lead with how the visitor feels right now — not with the therapy, the bees, or the science.
  • Tone is warm, pastoral, and human. Not clinical. Not corporate. Not preachy.
  • Bryan's faith is real and present — express it through warmth, care, and action. Avoid religious language that could feel exclusive to visitors who are not churchgoers.
  • The grief/PTSD/suicide-loss audience is welcome and should see themselves clearly on this site. Do not soften or hide this to appeal only to stressed professionals.
  • Jo's House is a 501(c)(3). State this clearly in the footer and About page — it builds trust.
  • Testimonials are placeholder — do not fabricate them. Mark them clearly as [Testimonial — placeholder].
  • Every page has one primary CTA. Do not stack competing actions.
  • The free course is a warm on-ramp for people not yet ready to book. Treat it as equally important to the booking CTA.
  • DO use the exact colors, fonts, and spacing in 02-design-direction.md
  • DO make every page mobile-first and fully responsive
  • DO NOT add sections, pages, or features not listed in 04-structure.md
  • DO NOT use Lorem Ipsum anywhere
  • DO NOT use stock photos of smiling professionals — use real farm/nature photography
