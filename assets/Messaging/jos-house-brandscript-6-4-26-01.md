# BrandScript — Jo's House

---

## Controlling Idea

When the world won't let you rest, nature has an answer — and it's been here all along.

---

## 01 — A Character

**Who they are:** She's the kind of person who has read all the articles, downloaded the meditation apps, and still lies awake at 11pm with her mind running through tomorrow's list. She works hard, carries a lot, and takes pride in handling things. But lately, handling things feels like an uphill battle. She's not in crisis. She's just quietly exhausted, and she can't remember the last time she felt genuinely still.

**What they want:** Real rest. Not the kind that comes from a glass of wine or a Sunday nap, but a deep, full-body quiet she hasn't felt in years.

**Identity they're reaching for:** Someone who finally feels like herself again.

---

## 02 — With a Problem

**The villain:** A culture of overstimulation that has conditioned her nervous system to stay permanently on guard, and a wellness industry that keeps offering more things to do instead of space to simply be.

**External:** She can't find a genuine escape. Every option available locally is either clinical, generic, or just another version of something she's already tried.

**Internal:** "My mind won't shut off and I don't know how to make it stop." She's tired of trying things that don't work, and quietly afraid that real rest just isn't available to her anymore.

**Philosophical:** A person shouldn't have to drive four hours and compete for a booking just to find peace. Rest shouldn't be this hard to come by.

**The story they tell themselves:** I must be broken. Everyone else seems to manage fine. Maybe I'm just not someone who gets to feel calm.

---

## 03 — Meets a Guide

**Empathy statement:** Bryan knows what it feels like when nothing works. He's sat with grieving families, walked alongside people in crisis, and fought his own battles with stress and tension, until he found something that conventional medicine and therapy simply couldn't give him. He understands, from the inside, what it means to feel like you are out of options.

**Authority statement:** As a Sheriff's Department Chaplain, Bryan has been present for some of the hardest moments people face. He brought that same care and curiosity to discovering bee bed therapy, one of the most scientifically supported yet still rare holistic practices in the world. Jo's House is the only place in northeast Indiana to offer it, backed by real results and the testimonials of people right here in Whitley County who've experienced what's possible.

---

## 04 — Who Gives Them a Plan

**Step 1 — Book Your Session:** Head to our website and book your session online in just a few minutes. Pick your time, complete your booking, and you're all set.

**Step 2 — Learn:** Before you arrive, we'll send you everything you need to know. You'll also get a personal introduction to the bees and a short orientation when you show up, so you feel comfortable and ready to get the most out of your time.

**Step 3 — Rest:** Lie back on the bee bed for 45 minutes and let the hum, warmth, gentle vibration, and deep, sweet and earthy aroma of the hive do what it's been doing for centuries. Walk out feeling different than you walked in.

---

## 05 — And Calls Them to Action

**Direct CTA:** Book Your Session

**Transitional CTA:** Download the Free Course. Learn how bee beds work, hear from people who've experienced them, and talk to Bryan before you commit to anything.

**Affirmation statement:** You don't have to keep pushing through. Rest is closer than you think.

---

## 06A — That Ends in Success

A session at Jo's House isn't just an hour out of your day. It's the beginning of something different. You walk out with a body that's finally had a chance to exhale. The mental noise that follows you everywhere starts to quiet down. Sleep comes easier. The tension you've been carrying in your shoulders, your jaw, your chest begins to loosen. You get a full tour of the farm, a chance to connect with nature, and a sense of peace that catches you a little off guard with how good it feels. And maybe most importantly, you leave knowing that real rest is available to you. You're not broken. You just needed the right place to finally let go.

---

## 06B — And Helps Them Avoid Failure

Without a real reset, stress doesn't stay the same. It grows. The mind that won't quiet down today gets louder tomorrow. The body that can't release tension starts to carry it in ways that show up physically. Relationships absorb what we can't process on our own. And the longer someone goes without finding something that actually works, the easier it is to settle into the belief that this is just how life feels. That peace is something other people get to have. That's too high a price to pay for doing nothing.

---

## 07 — Identity Transformation

**From:** Tired, overstimulated, and quietly convinced there's no good answer for what she's carrying. She's tried the pills, the massages, the talking sessions, the apps, and she's running out of things to try and patience for trying them.

**To:** A woman who finally feels like herself again. She knows what it feels like to be at peace in her own body, and she knows where to go when she needs to find that again.

---

## Research Notes

Competitors in this space lead with the therapy itself, the bees, the vibrations, the science, and none of them speak to who the customer is or how they feel. Jo's House has room to own the transformation story that nobody else is telling. Audience research surfaced phrases like "trapped in my own mind" and "I've tried everything" as the real language of this customer, and those shaped the internal problem and identity sections. Bryan's faith-driven, family-oriented voice is also completely absent from every competing brand, which makes his personal story and the warmth of the farm genuine differentiators, not just personality details.
